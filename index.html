01

First, the water

Right on the edge of the Knysna lagoon.

Nest lives in The Boatshed, a 1922 boatbuilding shed on Thesen Island, now part of The Lofts Boutique Hotel. Step onto the deck and the estuary opens up in front of you: moored yachts, the Heads in the distance and the tide coming and going under your table.

Locals come for coffee and stay for lunch. Visitors find us by accident and come back on purpose.

02

Then, step inside

Trees growing indoors, warm light overhead.

High ceilings, old timber and real trees reaching for the skylights. Long tables for friends who arrive in a group, and quiet corners for a book and a flat white.

03

Past the pass

Cooking with the Nest.

Everything starts in our open kitchen: smoked brisket, slow braises, bread from île de païn next door and coffee pulled from the moment the doors open.

  • Breakfast 08:00 to 11:00
  • Lunch and dinner 11:30 to 20:00
  • Coffee and cake all day

04

The rhythm of the week

Two days the regulars plan around.

Every Wednesday

Mussel Pot Wednesday

Knysna mussels in a steaming pot, bread ends for the sauce, and the lagoon they came from right there in front of you. It runs till the pots are gone.

Every Saturday

Waffle Saturday

Golden waffles with berries and cream, from breakfast. Bring the kids, bring your appetite, and get here early.

Stay a little longer
